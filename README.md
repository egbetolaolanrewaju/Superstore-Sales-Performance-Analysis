# Superstore Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Superstore Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes sales performance, profitability, customer contribution, product categories, customer segments, sales trends, and geographic performance across the United States from **2014 to 2017**.

The objective is to transform transactional sales data into actionable business insights that can support sales, marketing, and strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions:

- How much revenue and profit did the business generate?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- Which product categories generate the highest profit?
- Which customers contribute the most profit?
- Which customer segments generate the highest sales?
- How have sales changed between 2014 and 2017?
- Which states contribute most to profitability?
- How does business performance vary across different years and categories?

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides the following headline metrics:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | $2.30M |
| Total Profit | $286.40K |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |
| Total Orders | 5K |
| Total Customers | 793 |

The analysis indicates an overall profit margin of approximately **12.7%**, meaning the business retained about $0.13 in profit for every $1.00 of sales.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Top 10 Customers by Profit

The dashboard identifies the customers generating the highest profit contribution.

This analysis helps businesses:

- Identify high-value customers
- Understand customer profitability
- Prioritize customer retention
- Support targeted sales strategies

---

### 2. Profit by Category

Profitability is analyzed across three major product categories:

- Technology
- Office Supplies
- Furniture

Technology generated the highest profit contribution, followed by Office Supplies and Furniture.

---

### 3. Sales by Customer Segment

Sales are segmented into:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

This provides insight into the contribution of different customer groups to overall sales performance.

---

### 4. Sales Trend Analysis

The dashboard tracks sales performance from **2014 to 2017**.

| Year | Sales |
|---|---:|
| 2014 | $0.48M |
| 2015 | $0.47M |
| 2016 | $0.61M |
| 2017 | $0.73M |

The analysis shows strong sales growth between 2015 and 2017, with 2017 recording the highest sales among the periods analyzed.

---

### 5. Geographic Profitability

A geographic visualization is used to analyze profit performance across U.S. states.

This helps identify geographic markets with stronger or weaker profitability and can support regional sales and business strategy.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- Total sales reached approximately **$2.30M**.
- The business generated approximately **$286.40K in profit**.
- Overall profit margin was approximately **12.47%**.
- Technology was the most profitable product category.
- Consumer customers generated the largest share of sales.
- Sales increased significantly from 2015 through 2017.
- 2017 recorded the strongest annual sales performance at approximately **$0.73M**.
- Customer-level profitability analysis highlights the most valuable customers for potential retention strategies.
- Geographic analysis provides visibility into profit performance across U.S. states.
